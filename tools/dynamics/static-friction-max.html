F_{s,max} = μ_s·N

Determine the maximum static friction force that can act before an object begins to slide, from the coefficient of static friction and the normal force.

📐 计算公式
max = μ_s·N。 静摩擦系数通常 > 动摩擦。

物体即将滑动时的静摩擦力上限。
