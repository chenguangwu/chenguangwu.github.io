🧪 High-Altitude Hypoxia Adaptation Simulator

A high-altitude hypoxia adaptation simulator: model blood-oxygen and ventilation changes at different altitudes to understand altitude-adaptation physiology for training education.

📐 工作原理与说明
伯努利试验蒙特卡洛模拟：每次试验独立以概率 p 判定成功，进行 n 次；实际频率 = 成功次数 / n；理论概率为 p；实际频率与理论值之差即抽样偏差，其标准误为 sqrt(p*(1-p)/n)，随 n 增大而减小。
