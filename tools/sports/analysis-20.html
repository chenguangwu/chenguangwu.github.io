🎯 Shooting Score & Stability Analysis

Enter each shot’s ring score to compute the mean, within-group spread and stability metrics, then generate a score trend for shooting-training review.

📐 工作原理与说明
样本标准差 SD = √[ Σ(xᵢ − x̄)² ÷ (n − 1) ]
变异系数 CV = SD ÷ 平均环值 × 100%

Enter every shot value. Total, mean, best, worst and range describe accuracy, while the sample standard deviation SD and coefficient of variation CV describe how repeatable the group is. Read them together: a high average with a large SD means the result came from luck, so work on a repeatable position rather than chasing single high shots. Pick the scoring system first since it changes the reference values. Everything is computed locally.

❓ 常见问题（FAQ）

环数还是分组？
分组（密集度）比总环更能反映稳定性。
环数高但散布大说明什么？
说明偶然性好、一致性差，应练姿势固化而非追单发高环，比赛看总环与稳定。
10 环制和 10.9 环制该怎么看？
两种制式的绝对环值不能直接比较。10.9 制把每环再细分为十个小数位，同样的一组成绩在 10.9 制下总环更高。跨制式比较稳定性时看变异系数 CV 更合适，它把标准差除以平均环消除了量纲差异。
