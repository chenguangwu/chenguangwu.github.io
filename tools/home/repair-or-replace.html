家电维修还是换新决策计算器

Compare repair and replacement for a home appliance using type, age, repair quote, new price, new-machine life and electricity savings; weigh annual cost, remaining safe life and fault risk to recommend repair, borderline or replace.

📐 Formula

Remaining safe life = reference safe life − used years; post-repair usable life = min(repair life, remaining safe life, or repair life if already past the limit); repair annual cost = repair quote ÷ post-repair usable life; new annual cost = new price ÷ new-machine life − annual electricity savings. Safety risk takes priority, then annual costs are compared.

🧮 Inputs

The figure in brackets is a common industry/maker reference life, used only for economic-life estimation; it is not a safety diagnosis or a mandatory scrap date for this unit.

Enter 0 if unknown; estimate from bills when the efficiency gap is large.

📌 Notes

  • Remaining safe life uses common industry and maker reference limits, not a mandatory scrap date; after the limit, check cords, plugs, grounding and residual-current protection.
  • Post-repair life takes the smaller of repair life and remaining safe life; past the limit it is not discounted further but raises the risk level.
  • Enter only the annual electricity saved by the new machine versus the old one; use 0 without measurements and do not enter the total bill.
  • Results are decision and budget references, not a substitute for maker service, an electrician or a gas safety check. Stop use immediately if there is leakage, smoke or a gas alarm.

📚 Deep Dive: Three Lines for Repair or Replace

Safety first. For gas water heaters, storage water heaters and high-power air conditioners, leakage, smoke, a gas alarm or repeated tripping should stop cost-only thinking: stop using it and have it inspected professionally.

Second, annual cost. Repair quote divided by post-repair years gives repair cost per year; new price divided by life, minus annual electricity savings, gives new cost per year. When they are close, add repair warranty, call-out visits and downtime.

Third, future failure odds. Fixing one part of an old appliance does not stop other parts from failing; repeated faults, a quote above half the new price, or less than one year of safe life left usually favor replacement.

❓ FAQ

维修报价多少算贵？
常用经验值是超过新机价格的一半就偏向换新；但还要看维修后能用多久。600 元修好还能用 4 年，每年 150 元；3000 元新机用 10 年，每年 300 元，维修仍可能更划算。
已超安全年限但机器还能用，修不修？
普通小家电可结合故障性质与维修质保判断；燃气、承压、大功率或出现漏电、冒烟、跳闸的家电，安全优先，不建议继续投入维修。
新机省电费怎么估？
用旧机与同规格一级能效新机的年耗电差乘以电价；没有实测数据就填 0，避免把总电费当成节省，导致换新被高估。
维修后预计再用年限填多少？
按维修师傅的判断或同类故障的常见寿命填写，并用安全剩余年限封顶；拿不准时取保守值 1–2 年，避免把维修算得过划算。
