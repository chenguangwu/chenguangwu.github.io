热水器容量与升数选型计算器

Size a water heater from household size, simultaneous showers, shower minutes and shower flow: estimate mixed and 60°C hot-water demand, then recommend storage tank capacity, gas heater flow rating, instantaneous power and breaker, plus daily hot-water electricity cost.

📐 Formula

Mixed water = simultaneous showers × shower minutes × shower flow; hot fraction = (target temperature − inlet temperature) ÷ (tank temperature − inlet temperature); hot water at the tank temperature (default 60°C) = mixed water × hot fraction; storage capacity = 60°C hot water ÷ usable fraction (default 80%); gas rating is normalized to a 25°C rise; instantaneous power = shower flow × 4.186 × temperature rise ÷ 60.

🧮 Inputs

Use 2 for two simultaneous showers; 1 for staggered showers needs a smaller tank.

Standard showerheads use about 6–10 L/min; high-pressure models can reach 12.

Storage units are commonly 2–3 kW; enter 6–24 kW for instantaneous models from the nameplate.

📌 Notes

  • Storage capacity assumes 60°C water and 80% usable volume; actual usable hot water depends on tank design, inlet temperature, mixing valve and insulation, so go one size up in winter.
  • Gas ratings are at a 25°C rise, not actual outlet flow; with 5°C inlet and 40°C target the rise is 35°C, so actual flow is roughly rated × 25 ÷ 35.
  • Instantaneous power assumes all electricity becomes heat and ignores efficiency and line losses; a licensed electrician must verify the breaker and wire size against the nameplate and service capacity.
  • Daily cost assumes one full heat per day for the household; standby, time-of-use tariffs and mixing ratios will change it.

📚 Deep Dive: Capacity, Flow Rating and Power

For storage, start with simultaneous showers, then the gap between users. Two 10-minute showers at 8 L/min need about 160 L mixed water at 40°C, about 89 L of 60°C water, and about 111 L tank volume at 80% usable, so choose 120 L or stagger users with an 80 L tank.

Gas ratings assume a 25°C rise. With 5°C inlet and 40°C target the rise is 35°C, so a 16 L/min unit delivers only about 11.4 L/min; two simultaneous showers need a larger rating based on total flow.

For instantaneous electric units, power and wiring matter more than capacity. 8 L/min at a 25°C rise needs about 14 kW, roughly 63 A at 220 V, usually an 80 A breaker and 10 mm² dedicated line; when service capacity is limited, storage or gas is often more practical.

❓ FAQ

储水式 60 L 够几个人洗？
按 40°C 混水、进水 15°C、储水 60°C 估算，60 L 约可供 1 人洗 10–12 分钟；两人连续洗要等加热，冬季建议 80 L 起。
燃气 12 L 和 16 L 差多少？
差在温升 25°C 时的额定流量。单卫 8 L/min 淋浴 12 L 足够；双卫同时开、或冬季进水很冷时，16 L 以上更稳。
即热式一定要改电吗？
多数情况要。8 L/min 淋浴约 14 kW，普通 16 A 插座带不动；需独立专线、专用空开，并由电工核对入户容量与线径。
容量越大越费电吗？
加热同样水量所需能量接近，大容量主要增加保温散热与占地。按人数选合适容量，比盲目加大更省电。
