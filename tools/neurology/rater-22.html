Rater 22

Rater 22 - online utility on ToolBox.

SARA 小脑共济失调量表总分 = 8 项检查（步态/站姿/坐姿/言语/指鼻/跟膝胫/轮替/跟-胫滑动）之和，满分 40；≤3 轻度、≤8 轻中度、≤15 中度、≤30 重度、>30 极重度。
Per-item scores, total, severity, and interpretation can be exported as CSV

Formula & Standard References

Item Formula Source-Standard
Scale structure SARA has 8 items with a total range of 0-40 Schmitz-Hubsch T et al. Mov Disord 2006;21:589-594
Gait and stance Gait scores 0-8 and stance 0-6, carrying the highest weight Schmitz-Hubsch T et al. Mov Disord 2006;21:589-594
Severity reading A higher score means more severe ataxia; 0 means no ataxia Schmitz-Hubsch T et al. Mov Disord 2006;21:589-594
⚠️ 使用说明与注意事项
  • SARA适用于各种类型小脑性共济失调评估
  • 步态评估需在平坦地面行走10米(含转身)
  • 0-3轻度 4-8轻中度 9-15中度 16-30重度 >30极重度
  • 常用于疾病进展监测和临床试验疗效评估
  • 本工具纯前端运行，数据不会上传到服务器
