Manning Open Channel Flow Calculator

Compute open-channel uniform-flow discharge Q = (1/n)*A*R^(2/3)*sqrt(S) from roughness n, cross-sectional area A, hydraulic radius R and bed slope S.

📐 计算公式
曼宁公式明渠流量（本页代码口径，主输出为流量）：Q=(1÷n)×A×R^(2÷3)×√S（m³/s，n 糙率、A 过水断面 m²、R 水力半径 m、S 底坡）；联动量：平均流速 v=Q÷A（m/s，派生量）、Chezy 系数 C=R^(1÷6)÷n。校验：四字段经输入守卫必填且须为合法数值，否则提示并中止。判据：无固定分级，Q 与断面面积成正比、与糙率成反比、随底坡平方根增长。

由糙率 n、过水断面 A、水力半径 R 与底坡 S，按 Q=(1/n)A·R^(2/3)·√S 计算明渠均匀流流量。

📌 计算说明
  • 水力半径 R = A/P（P 为湿周）。
  • 适用均匀流；n 取值：混凝土 0.012–0.015，天然河道 0.025–0.035。

Constants and standards used on this page with their authoritative sources, for verification and citation.

US customary coefficient 1.486 (often written 1.49) ft^(1/3)/s Chow V.T. (1959), Open-Channel Hydraulics
Roughness n (concrete) 0.012–0.017 s/m^(1/3) Chow (1959) Table 5-6; common values in Chinese hydraulics texts
Roughness n (earth canal / natural channel) 0.018–0.030 / 0.030–0.050 s/m^(1/3) Chow (1959); GB 50286-2013 levee design code

Note: the Manning equation applies to uniform turbulent open-channel flow. n is empirical and depends on bed material, vegetation, irregularity and stage; select it from site survey and design codes. The coefficient 1.0 applies only in SI units.

标准算例
n=0.013、R=0.5、S=0.001 → v=(1/0.013)×0.5^0.667×0.001^0.5≈1.43 m/s；Q=v·A。
底坡影响
S 翻倍到 0.002，v 增约 41%，同断面过流能力明显提高。

❓ 常见问题（FAQ）

R 是什么？
水力半径=过水面积/湿周，宽浅渠近似水深，圆管满流为 D/4。
n 怎么取？
混凝土 0.012~0.015、土渠 0.02~0.03、植被渠更大，按护面查表。
