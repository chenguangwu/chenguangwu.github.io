🖼 Image to ASCII Art Converter

Map image brightness to ASCII characters with adjustable width, contrast, brightness and character set, previewed and copied locally.

📐 计算公式 / 原理
亮度 L = 0.299R + 0.587G + 0.114B；字符索引 i = round(L / 255 × (n − 1))
基于 BT.601 亮度加权：把每个采样块的平均亮度映射到字符集（如 "@%#*+=-:. "）的对应档位；输出宽度、对比度、亮度与字符集均可调，用于生成字符画。
