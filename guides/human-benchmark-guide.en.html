Human Benchmark

Human Benchmark bundles quick challenges — reaction time, memory, verbal fluency and accuracy — so you can compare your performance against yourself and rough population averages.

  • Key Features
  • Use Cases

Key Features

  • Reaction-time test
  • Memory and sequence challenges
  • Accuracy tasks
  • Self-comparison over time
  • 100% client-side

Use Cases

  • Casual cognitive warm-up
  • Tracking reaction time
  • Fun self-experiment

How to Use

  1. Pick a challenge
  2. Follow the on-screen prompt
  3. Respond as fast and accurately as you can
  4. Review your stats
  5. Retest to compare

Practical Tips

  • Use a wired connection for reaction tests
  • Fatigue lowers scores
  • Do a few practice rounds
  • Compare trends, not single runs

FAQ

Are scores comparable to others?
They are self-referential; population numbers are rough averages, not a diagnosis.
Why does reaction time vary?
Sleep, device input lag and attention all shift reaction time significantly.
